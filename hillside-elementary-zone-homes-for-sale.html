Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Hillside zone covers west Amarillo west of Soncy Road, from Hillside Road north to I-40, and runs west into rural land. It is an Amarillo address in Canyon ISD.

Homes range from late-2000s builds to brand-new construction, with active building in Town Square and Homestead.

Neighborhoods in the zone

  • Hillside Terrace
  • Town Square
  • Homestead

Most of Homestead is in the Hillside zone; a few homes are in Amarillo ISD.

Hillside Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Hillside Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD, even though many homes in the zone have Amarillo addresses.

What high school does Hillside Elementary feed into?

West Plains High School. Students go from Hillside Elementary to Greenways Intermediate for 5th and 6th grade, then West Plains Junior High, then West Plains High School.

Where is Hillside Elementary?

9600 Perry Ave, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Hillside Elementary?

Not right now. Canyon ISD is closed to transfers, so the address decides the school. More in our school zone FAQ.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Hillside Elementary?

All or part of Hillside Terrace, Town Square and Homestead. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Zones on the Same Path

Other elementary zones that feed West Plains High School: Arden Road Elementary, Heritage Hills Elementary and Spring Canyon Elementary (north part of its zone). Part of the Lakeview Elementary zone does too.
