Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Spring Canyon zone covers the area between Amarillo and Canyon, mostly west of I-27, and runs west into rural Randall County. The northern part of the zone goes on to the West Plains schools and the southern part, closer to Canyon, goes on to the Canyon schools. Homes have either Amarillo or Canyon addresses.

Homes include acreage properties built from the late 1980s through the 2000s, and a lot of new construction in recent years.

Strawberry Fields, Las Casas and Wildflower/Maple Fields are split with Lakeview Elementary.

Where the Zone Splits

Rockwell Road is the dividing line after elementary school. Homes north of Rockwell Road go on to Greenways Intermediate, West Plains Junior High and West Plains High. Homes south of Rockwell Road go on to Canyon Intermediate, Canyon Junior High and Canyon High.

Homes right along the line should be checked by address on the map above.

Spring Canyon Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Spring Canyon Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD.

What high school does Spring Canyon Elementary feed into?

It depends on the address. The northern part of the zone goes to West Plains High and the southern part goes to Canyon High. Check a specific address on our school zone map.

Where is Spring Canyon Elementary?

20 Buckston Dr, Canyon.

Can my child transfer into Spring Canyon Elementary?

Not right now. Canyon ISD is closed to transfers, so the address decides the school. More in our school zone FAQ.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Spring Canyon Elementary?

All or part of Spring Canyon, Spring Lake, Hunter Estates, Dove Prairie/Eagle Point, Mesquite Ridge, Strawberry Fields, Las Casas and Wildflower/Maple Fields.

Other Zones on the Same Path

Other elementary zones that feed West Plains High School: Arden Road Elementary, Heritage Hills Elementary and Hillside Elementary. Part of the Lakeview Elementary zone does too.
