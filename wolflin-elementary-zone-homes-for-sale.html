Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Wolflin zone covers the area just south of I-40, from Western Street east to I-27 (Canyon Expressway), down to around SW 26th Avenue.

Homes range from historic 1910s and 1920s homes near Oliver-Eakle to 1950s and 1960s homes in Wolflin, plus 1960s condos.

Wolflin Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Wolflin Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Amarillo ISD.

What middle school and high school does Wolflin Elementary feed into?

Tascosa High School. The middle school is Austin Middle School.

Where is Wolflin Elementary?

2026 S Hughes St, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Wolflin Elementary?

Amarillo ISD accepts transfer requests when a campus has room. Requests turned in by May 1 get priority, an approved transfer is good for one school year, and families provide their own transportation. See AISD's transfer page. Buying inside the zone is the sure way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Wolflin Elementary?

All or part of Wolflin and Oliver Eakle. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Southwest Amarillo Elementary Zones for Tascosa High

These elementary zones in our area also feed Tascosa High School: Coronado Elementary, Olsen Park Elementary and Paramount Terrace Elementary.
