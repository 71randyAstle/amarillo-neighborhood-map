Homes for Sale in the Lakeview Elementary Zone

Lakeview Elementary is a Canyon ISD school south of Amarillo. Homes in its attendance zone feed into either the West Plains schools (Greenways Intermediate, West Plains Junior High and West Plains High) or the Randall schools (Pinnacle Intermediate, Randall Junior High and Randall High), depending on the address.

Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Lakeview zone is south of Amarillo, between the city and Canyon, on both sides of I-27. Much of it is acreage and rural land, along with newer neighborhoods like Strawberry Fields. It is Canyon ISD.

Homes include acreage properties from the 1960s through the 1980s and newer homes built from the mid-2010s on in neighborhoods like Strawberry Fields.

Neighborhoods in the zone

  • Strawberry Fields
  • Las Casas
  • Wildflower/Maple Fields

Las Casas and Wildflower/Maple Fields are split with Spring Canyon Elementary.

Lakeview Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Lakeview Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD, even though many homes in the zone have Amarillo addresses.

What high school does Lakeview Elementary feed into?

It depends on the address. Part of the zone goes to Greenways Intermediate, West Plains Junior High and West Plains High, and part goes to Pinnacle Intermediate, Randall Junior High and Randall High. Check a specific address on our school zone map.

Where is Lakeview Elementary?

6407 Lair Rd, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Lakeview Elementary?

Not right now. Canyon ISD's transfer page says the district is closed to out-of-district transfers and has been closed to in-district transfers since the 2023-24 school year, so buying inside the zone is the way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Lakeview Elementary?

All or part of Strawberry Fields, Las Casas and Wildflower/Maple Fields. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.
