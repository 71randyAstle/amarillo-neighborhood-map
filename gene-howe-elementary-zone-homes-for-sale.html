Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Gene Howe zone has two parts. One is a section of south Amarillo between I-27 (Canyon Drive) and Western Street from Hollywood Road (Loop 335) north to Farmers Avenue, reaching east to Georgia Street up to SW 58th Avenue. The other, much larger part covers southeast Amarillo east of I-27 and runs out into rural land east and southeast of the city. Both parts are Canyon ISD.

In the southwest Amarillo part of the zone, most homes date from the 1960s through the 1980s, with a few newer homes mixed in.

Gene Howe Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Gene Howe Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD, even though many homes in the zone have Amarillo addresses.

What high school does Gene Howe Elementary feed into?

Randall High School. Students go from Gene Howe Elementary to Pinnacle Intermediate for 5th and 6th grade, then Randall Junior High, then Randall High School.

Where is Gene Howe Elementary?

5108 Pico Blvd, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Gene Howe Elementary?

Not right now. Canyon ISD's transfer page says the district is closed to out-of-district transfers and has been closed to in-district transfers since the 2023-24 school year, so buying inside the zone is the way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Gene Howe Elementary?

All or part of Hollywood/Scotsman and Lonesome Dove. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Randall Elementary Zones

These elementary zones also feed Randall High School: City View Elementary, Sundown Lane Elementary and Lakeview Elementary (part of its zone).
