Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The South Georgia zone covers the area east of I-27 (Canyon Expressway), from about SW 44th Avenue south to SW 58th Avenue.

Most homes were built from the mid-1950s through the 1970s.

Neighborhoods in the zone

  • McCarty/Georgia Shores
  • Southlawn

Southlawn is split with neighboring zones.

South Georgia Elementary Zone FAQ

Is South Georgia Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Amarillo ISD.

What middle school and high school does South Georgia Elementary feed into?

Amarillo High School for most of the zone, with some streets zoned to Caprock High School. The middle school is Fannin Middle School.

Where is South Georgia Elementary?

5018 Susan Dr, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into South Georgia Elementary?

Amarillo ISD accepts transfer requests when a campus has room. Requests turned in by May 1 get priority, an approved transfer is good for one school year, and families provide their own transportation. See AISD's transfer page. Buying inside the zone is the sure way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to South Georgia Elementary?

All or part of McCarty/Georgia Shores and Southlawn. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Southwest Amarillo Elementary Zones for Amarillo High

These elementary zones in our area also feed Amarillo High School: Belmar Elementary, Puckett Elementary, Ridgecrest Elementary, Sleepy Hollow Elementary, Western Plateau Elementary and Windsor Elementary.
