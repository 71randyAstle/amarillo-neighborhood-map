Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

Amarillo High School's zone covers much of southwest Amarillo, roughly between Western Street and Soncy Road from I-40 south to about Hillside Road and Farmers Avenue, plus an area east of I-27 around South Georgia Street.

Most homes were built from the 1960s through the 1990s, from Belmar, Ridgecrest and Western Plateau to Puckett, Windsor and Sleepy Hollow, with newer homes in the Colonies.

Amarillo High School Zone FAQ

Is Amarillo High School in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Amarillo ISD.

Which elementary schools feed Amarillo High School?

Belmar Elementary, Puckett Elementary, Ridgecrest Elementary, Sleepy Hollow Elementary, South Georgia Elementary, Western Plateau Elementary, Windsor Elementary, Paramount Terrace Elementary and Olsen Park Elementary. Some of those zones are split with other high schools, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Where is Amarillo High School?

4225 Danbury St, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Amarillo High School?

Amarillo ISD accepts transfer requests when a campus has room. Requests turned in by May 1 get priority, an approved transfer is good for one school year, and families provide their own transportation. See AISD's transfer page. Buying inside the zone is the sure way in.

Other High School Zones in Southwest Amarillo

West Plains High School and Randall High School.
