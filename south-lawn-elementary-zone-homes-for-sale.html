Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The South Lawn zone covers south Amarillo from about SW 44th Avenue south to SW 58th Avenue, east of the South Georgia zone toward Polk Street.

Homes include smaller 1950s homes and newer single-family homes from the 1980s through the early 2000s.

South Lawn Elementary Zone FAQ

Is South Lawn Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Amarillo ISD.

What middle school and high school does South Lawn Elementary feed into?

Caprock High School. The middle school is Fannin Middle School.

Where is South Lawn Elementary?

4719 S Bowie St, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into South Lawn Elementary?

Amarillo ISD accepts transfer requests when a campus has room. Requests turned in by May 1 get priority, an approved transfer is good for one school year, and families provide their own transportation. See AISD's transfer page. Buying inside the zone is the sure way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to South Lawn Elementary?

Southlawn. Zone lines can change, so check a specific address on our school zone map.
