Homes for Sale in the Olsen Park Elementary Zone

Olsen Park Elementary is an Amarillo ISD school in southwest Amarillo. Most homes in its attendance zone go on to Crockett Middle School and Tascosa High School, but some streets are zoned to Houston Middle and Amarillo High School instead.

Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Olsen Park zone covers the area between Western Street and Bell Street, from SW 34th Avenue north to Plains Boulevard.

Most homes date from the mid-1950s to the early 1960s, with a few from the 1980s.

Neighborhoods in the zone

  • Olsen
  • Avondale

Avondale is mostly in the Avondale Elementary zone.

Olsen Park Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Olsen Park Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Amarillo ISD.

What middle school and high school does Olsen Park Elementary feed into?

Tascosa High School for most of the zone, with some streets zoned to Amarillo High School. The middle school is Crockett Middle School for most homes, with some streets zoned to Houston Middle.

Where is Olsen Park Elementary?

2409 Emil Ave, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Olsen Park Elementary?

Amarillo ISD accepts transfer requests when a campus has room. Requests turned in by May 1 get priority, an approved transfer is good for one school year, and families provide their own transportation. See AISD's transfer page. Buying inside the zone is the sure way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Olsen Park Elementary?

All or part of Olsen and Avondale. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Southwest Amarillo Elementary Zones for Tascosa High

These elementary zones in our area also feed Tascosa High School: Coronado Elementary, Paramount Terrace Elementary and Wolflin Elementary.
