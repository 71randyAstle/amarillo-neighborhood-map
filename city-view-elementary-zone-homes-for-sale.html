Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The City View zone covers south Amarillo from Hollywood Road (Loop 335) north to about SW 58th Avenue. Western Street is its western edge south of Farmers Avenue, and north of Farmers it sits east of Georgia Street. It is an Amarillo address in Canyon ISD, not Amarillo ISD.

Most homes here were built from the mid-2000s on, with some from the late 1990s and a good share of newer construction in recent years.

City View Elementary Zone FAQ

Is City View Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD, even though many homes in the zone have Amarillo addresses.

What high school does City View Elementary feed into?

Randall High School. Students go from City View Elementary to Pinnacle Intermediate for 5th and 6th grade, then Randall Junior High, then Randall High School.

Where is City View Elementary?

3400 Knoll Dr, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into City View Elementary?

Not right now. Canyon ISD's transfer page says the district is closed to out-of-district transfers and has been closed to in-district transfers since the 2023-24 school year, so buying inside the zone is the way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to City View Elementary?

All or part of City View, Foxridge/Pheasant Run, Saturn Terrace and The Meadows. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Randall Elementary Zones

These elementary zones also feed Randall High School: Gene Howe Elementary, Sundown Lane Elementary and Lakeview Elementary (part of its zone).
