Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Sundown Lane zone covers south Amarillo south of Hollywood Road (Loop 335), from around Bell Street east across I-27, then southeast out to Lake Tanglewood and Timbercreek Canyon. It is Canyon ISD.

Homes range from 1960s to 1980s lake homes around Lake Tanglewood, to early-1990s homes, to newer homes from the 2010s and 2020s in Pinnacle, River Falls and Beacon Pointe.

Sundown Lane Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Sundown Lane Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD, even though many homes in the zone have Amarillo addresses.

What high school does Sundown Lane Elementary feed into?

Randall High School. Students go from Sundown Lane Elementary to Pinnacle Intermediate for 5th and 6th grade, then Randall Junior High, then Randall High School.

Where is Sundown Lane Elementary?

4546 Sundown Ln, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Sundown Lane Elementary?

Not right now. Canyon ISD is closed to transfers, so the address decides the school. More in our school zone FAQ.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Sundown Lane Elementary?

All or part of Lake Tanglewood, Pinnacle, River Falls, Timbercreek Canyon and Beacon Pointe.

Other Zones on the Same Path

Other elementary zones that feed Randall High School: City View Elementary. So do Gene Howe Elementary and part of Lakeview Elementary.
