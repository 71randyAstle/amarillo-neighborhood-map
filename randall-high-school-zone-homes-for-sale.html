Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

Randall High School's zone covers south and southeast Amarillo, including the area around I-27 south of SW 58th Avenue and the land south of Hollywood Road (Loop 335), out to Lake Tanglewood, Timbercreek Canyon and rural land southeast of the city. It is Canyon ISD, even though most homes have Amarillo addresses.

Homes range from 1960s and 1970s homes in older south Amarillo neighborhoods and lake homes at Lake Tanglewood, to newer homes in City View, The Meadows, Pinnacle, River Falls and Beacon Pointe.

Randall High School Zone FAQ

Is Randall High School in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD.

Which elementary schools feed Randall High School?

City View Elementary and Sundown Lane Elementary. Gene Howe Elementary and part of the Lakeview Elementary zone also feed Randall. Some zones are split with other high schools, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Where is Randall High School?

9201 S Bell St, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Randall High School?

Not right now. Canyon ISD is closed to transfers, so the address decides the school. More in our school zone FAQ.
