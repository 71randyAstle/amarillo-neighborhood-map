Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Woodlands zone covers northwest Amarillo, roughly between Coulter Street and the Western Street area from around Amarillo Boulevard north, including the Tascosa Country Club and La Paloma area.

Homes range from 1980s homes in Westcliff, to 1990s and 2000s homes around La Paloma and Tascosa Country Club, to 2010s homes in The Vineyards and new construction in The Trails.

Woodlands Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Woodlands Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Amarillo ISD.

What middle school and high school does Woodlands Elementary feed into?

De Zavala Middle School for 5th through 8th grade, then Tascosa High School.

Where is Woodlands Elementary?

2501 N Coulter St, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Woodlands Elementary?

Amarillo ISD takes transfer requests when a campus has room, but buying in the zone is the sure way in. More in our school zone FAQ.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Woodlands Elementary?

All or part of The Woodlands, La Paloma, Quail Creek, The Vineyards, Westcliff, Estancia and The Trails. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.
