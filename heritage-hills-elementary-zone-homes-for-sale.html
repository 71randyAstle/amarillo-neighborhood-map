Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Heritage Hills zone covers the far southwest edge of Amarillo, west of Soncy Road and south of Hillside Road, plus land south of Hollywood Road (Loop 335). It runs west into rural Randall County. It is an Amarillo address in Canyon ISD.

This is one of the newer parts of Amarillo. Most homes were built from the late 2010s on, and there is plenty of new construction in Heritage Hills, Highland Springs and Grey Hawk.

Heritage Hills Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Heritage Hills Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD, even though many homes in the zone have Amarillo addresses.

What high school does Heritage Hills Elementary feed into?

West Plains High School. Students go from Heritage Hills Elementary to Greenways Intermediate for 5th and 6th grade, then West Plains Junior High, then West Plains High School.

Where is Heritage Hills Elementary?

8200 Crestline Dr, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Heritage Hills Elementary?

Not right now. Canyon ISD is closed to transfers, so the address decides the school. More in our school zone FAQ.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Heritage Hills Elementary?

All or part of Heritage Hills, Highland Springs, Grey Hawk and Wedgewood. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Zones on the Same Path

Other elementary zones that feed West Plains High School: Arden Road Elementary, Hillside Elementary and Spring Canyon Elementary (north part of its zone). Part of the Lakeview Elementary zone does too.
