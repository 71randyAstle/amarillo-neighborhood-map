Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

The Coronado zone covers the area east of Western Street from about SW 26th Avenue south to I-27 (Canyon Expressway), including the streets around Paramount Boulevard and Olsen Boulevard.

Homes are mostly 1950s single-family homes, along with condos from the 1960s through the early 1980s.

Neighborhoods in the zone

  • Wolflin
  • Paramount

Wolflin and Paramount are split with neighboring zones.

Coronado Elementary Zone FAQ

Is Coronado Elementary in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Amarillo ISD.

What middle school and high school does Coronado Elementary feed into?

Tascosa High School. The middle school is Austin Middle School.

Where is Coronado Elementary?

3210 Wimberly Rd, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into Coronado Elementary?

Amarillo ISD accepts transfer requests when a campus has room. Requests turned in by May 1 get priority, an approved transfer is good for one school year, and families provide their own transportation. See AISD's transfer page. Buying inside the zone is the sure way in.

Which neighborhoods are zoned to Coronado Elementary?

All or part of Wolflin and Paramount. Zone lines can split a neighborhood, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Other Southwest Amarillo Elementary Zones for Tascosa High

These elementary zones in our area also feed Tascosa High School: Olsen Park Elementary, Paramount Terrace Elementary and Wolflin Elementary.
