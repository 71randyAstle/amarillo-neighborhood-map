The Meadows & Saturn Terrace Homes for Sale in South Amarillo

The Meadows and Saturn Terrace sit side by side in south Amarillo near Farmers Avenue and S Georgia Street, inside city limits and in Canyon ISD. Saturn Terrace is the older part, with homes built from about 2009 on space-themed streets like Voyager and Apollo. The Meadows is the newer part, built out in the early 2020s with somewhat larger homes.

What Buyers Ask About The Meadows & Saturn Terrace

What is the difference between The Meadows and Saturn Terrace?

They are two parts of the same area. Saturn Terrace is the older part, with homes built from about 2009, mostly 1,400 to 1,700 square feet. The Meadows is the newer part, built from 2021 to 2024, with homes mostly 1,650 to 2,050 square feet. The MLS lists homes in both under Saturn Terrace.

Do The Meadows and Saturn Terrace have an HOA?

HOA dues are not common here. Check the deed restrictions and HOA status for the specific property before you buy.

Why are there so few homes for sale here?

The Meadows and Saturn Terrace are small, so homes here don't come up for sale often. Setting up a new-listing alert is the easiest way to hear about the next one as soon as it hits the market.

Are The Meadows and Saturn Terrace in Amarillo ISD or Canyon ISD?

Canyon ISD. Even though they are inside Amarillo city limits, both are served by Canyon ISD schools. Check the specific address with Canyon ISD.

What are property taxes in The Meadows and Saturn Terrace?

Both are inside Amarillo city limits in Randall County. 2026 adopted tax rates per $100 of value:

  • Canyon ISD: 0.915400
  • City of Amarillo: 0.430700
  • Randall County: 0.400930
  • Amarillo College: 0.246700

Canyon ISD tax applies to value after the $140,000 school homestead exemption; non-homestead properties pay it on full value. HOA dues are separate and are not on the tax bill.

Tax rates are set each year by the taxing units and can change. Figures shown are for the 2026 tax year, are provided for general information only, and are not guaranteed. Actual taxes depend on each property's appraised value and exemptions. Verify exact amounts with the Potter-Randall Appraisal District or the Randall County Tax Office before buying.
