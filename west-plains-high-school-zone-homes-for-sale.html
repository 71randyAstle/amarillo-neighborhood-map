Where the Zone Is and What the Homes Are Like

West Plains High School's zone covers southwest and west Amarillo south of I-40, mostly west of Coulter Street and Soncy Road, plus the Arden Road area that reaches east to I-27 and rural land to the west and south toward Canyon. It is Canyon ISD, even though most homes have Amarillo addresses.

This is the newest part of Amarillo for housing. Homes run from 1970s and 1990s neighborhoods like Estacado and Westover to new construction in Heritage Hills, Town Square, Homestead and Greenways.

West Plains High School Zone FAQ

Is West Plains High School in Canyon ISD or Amarillo ISD?

Canyon ISD.

Which elementary schools feed West Plains High School?

Arden Road Elementary, Heritage Hills Elementary, Hillside Elementary and Spring Canyon Elementary (north part). Part of the Lakeview Elementary zone also feeds West Plains. Some zones are split with other high schools, so check a specific address on our school zone map.

Where is West Plains High School?

10576 Arden Rd, Amarillo.

Can my child transfer into West Plains High School?

Not right now. Canyon ISD is closed to transfers, so the address decides the school. More in our school zone FAQ.
